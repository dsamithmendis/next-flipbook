# Code of conduct

Be respectful and constructive. Assume good faith, keep feedback about the work, and welcome people of every background and experience level.

Not acceptable: harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, publishing others' private information, or sustained disruption of discussions.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits and issues that break these rules, and may block people who repeat the behaviour.

To report a problem, email **samithmendis.01@gmail.com**. Reports are handled privately.
